
-- Section 1: Basic Words & Three-Letter Words --
The cat sat on the mat.
A dog ran by.
Simple three-letter words: The, cat, dog, sky, sun, mom.
Longer words like elephant and house should not be matched by the 3-letter filter.

-- Section 2: Words Starting with a Vowel --
An elephant is in the room.
Apple trees, igloos, and orange groves can be seen.
Notice words like "An" and "is" that start with a vowel.

-- Section 3: Dates (DD/MM/YYYY) --
Important dates:
Today is 19/02/2025 and tomorrow is 20/02/2025.
Other dates: 07/04/2021, 31/12/2020.
For this demo, the valid date format is DD/MM/YYYY.

-- Section 4: Hello Lookahead --
Greetings:
hello, world! hello there hello? hello world
"hello" (case-insensitive) not immediately followed by punctuation should be matched.

-- Section 5: Currency Amounts --
Prices: $20, $15.99, and $100.
Budget items: Only $5, sometimes $0.99, or even $250.
Special discount: Save $10.00 on your next purchase!
